Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 13, 2026

PalmAura is a coming-soon palm reading app. This website is a static landing page and does not currently collect palm photos, create accounts, process payments, or run an in-browser reading experience.

Information we collect

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address and message. Basic server/CDN logs may be processed by Cloudflare to keep the website secure and available.

Website analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate website usage, such as pages viewed, referrers, approximate location, device/browser information, site interactions, and Google Analytics cookies or similar client identifiers. Google may process this analytics data according to its own privacy policy.

How we use information

We use messages you send and aggregate analytics to respond to you, manage early interest in PalmAura, understand what visitors read, and improve the product before launch.

Palm photos

This landing page does not accept or store palm photos. Before the app launches with photo uploads, PalmAura will publish a clearer product privacy notice describing how photos are handled.
