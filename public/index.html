What PalmAura returns

A palm reading that looks at your actual hand.

Instead of drawing a generic template, PalmAura uses the photo you provide. The app identifies the main palm lines, aligns the overlay to nearby visible creases, and writes a reading from the patterns it can see.

  • Heart line emotional style, loyalty, boundaries
  • Head line mental rhythm, decision-making, focus
  • Life line vitality, grounding, how you recover
  • Fate line momentum, work, timing, direction
