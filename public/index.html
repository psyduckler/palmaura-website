What PalmAura returns

A palm reading that looks at your actual hand.

Instead of drawing a generic template, PalmAura uses the photo you provide. The app identifies the main palm lines, aligns the overlay to nearby visible creases, and writes a reading from the patterns it can see.

  • Heart lineemotional style, loyalty, boundaries
  • Head linemental rhythm, decision-making, focus
  • Life linevitality, grounding, how you recover
  • Fate linemomentum, work, timing, direction

The Method

From hand to story in three steps.

  1. I

    Frame the palm

    On-device guidance checks for a clear, well-lit hand photo before a reading is requested.

  2. II

    Trace the lines

    AI identifies the major palm-line paths; local pixel snapping keeps the overlay attached to real creases.

  3. III

    Receive the story

    Your reading is written in a symbolic voice: direct enough to feel personal, mystical enough to be fun.

Why PalmAura
